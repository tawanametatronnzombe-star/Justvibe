What is Stress?

Stress is a natural response to demands in your life - it helps motivate you to get things done.

However, long periods of high stress can lead to feeling overwhelmed or burnt out. We can't avoid stress completely, but practice can help calm your body and mind.

Common Symptoms

💥 Headache
😬 Clenched Jaw
🌙 Loss of Sleep
💓 Racing Heart
💦 Sweating
⚡ Muscle Tension

🤝 Supporting Someone Who is Stressed

Your role doesn't need to be their therapist—simply being present is powerful. Try these strategies:

  • Write a to-do list together to help prioritize tasks.
  • Take a task off their plate by offering to help directly.
  • Share tools and strategies that have worked for you.
